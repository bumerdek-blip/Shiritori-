📖 How to Play

  • Each level starts with a bird's name
  • Your word must start with the last letter of the previous word (the bright gold tile)
  • Using more of the last 4 letters earns chain bonus points
  • Bird names score ×1.5 — longer words score more
  • Words must be at least 3 letters · 60 seconds per word
  • Miss the timer and the round restarts!
